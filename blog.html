Atelier Dialogue

Master Artisan Interview Series

Conversations with the master stonemasons and ceramicists who execute Verona installations across the globe.

Enzo Fabrizi Master Stonemason & Waterjet Calibration Director (38 Years Experience)
Q: What is the most common mistake architects make when detailing mega-slabs?

"Underestimating substrate flatness. A 3200mm porcelain slab cannot bend over floor humps. We mandate self-leveling compounds guaranteeing less than 2mm variance over a 3-meter straight edge. Once that plane is true, the slab sits eternal."

Q: How do you achieve razor-fine 1mm joints without risking edge chipping?

"Diamond chamfering. Every cut edge receives a 0.5mm micro-bevel at our atelier before packing. This relieves tension and ensures that during thermal expansion, adjacent slabs never bind or micro-fracture."
